Our Digital Services

Access learning resources and library services with ease.

1. E-Book Access

Explore digital books and enjoy reading on your computer, tablet, or mobile device.

Availability: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How to access: Log in using your library membership details and select an available e-book.

2. Online Book Catalogue

Search our book collection and check book titles, authors, and availability.

Availability: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM.

How to use: Enter a book title or author name in the catalogue search and browse the results.

3. Digital Research Resources

Access educational articles, reference materials, and online learning resources for academic research.

Availability: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

How to access: Visit the library and ask the staff for assistance with digital research resources.

4. Online Membership Support

Get assistance with membership information, renewals, and library account questions.

Availability: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.

How to use: Contact our library team through the contact page and provide your membership details.
