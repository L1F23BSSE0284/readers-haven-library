Our Membership Plans

Choose a membership plan that suits your reading needs.

1. Basic Membership

Annual Fee: PKR 2,000

Borrowing Limit: 2 books at a time

Loan Period: 14 days

Late Return Rule: PKR 20 fine per book per late day.

2. Standard Membership

Annual Fee: PKR 3,500

Borrowing Limit: 4 books at a time

Loan Period: 21 days

Late Return Rule: PKR 15 fine per book per late day.

3. Premium Membership

Annual Fee: PKR 5,000

Borrowing Limit: 6 books at a time

Loan Period: 30 days

Late Return Rule: PKR 10 fine per book per late day.

Membership Rules

  • Members must provide correct registration information.
  • Books must be returned on or before the due date.
  • Late return fines apply according to the selected plan.
  • Members are responsible for damaged or lost books.
  • Membership must be renewed annually.
